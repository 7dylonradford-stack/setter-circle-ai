WHAT WE OFFER

More than just training.

BEGINNER → FIRST ROLE

Step-by-step Training

Structured appointment-setting modules covering everything from fundamentals and conversation psychology through discovery, qualification, objections, booking, follow-up and performing in a real role.

40 DAY PROGRAMME

A clear daily roadmap

40 focused days of thoughtful tasks, measurable deliverables, assessments and progression designed to build the skills, habits and consistency needed to pursue your first setter role.

1-1 SUPPORT

Guidance when you need it

Personal support alongside the programme, with guidance when you're stuck, feedback on your development and a clear next action rather than guessing what to work on.

SETTER LAB

Realistic AI Practice

Train against varied AI prospects, practise discovery and objections, prepare for interviews and receive scored feedback that highlights your strengths and weakest skills.

COMMUNITY

PERSONALISED COACHING

WHO WE ARE

The Setter Circle

A complete appointment-setting mentorship designed to take students from beginner toward their first role through structured learning, deliberate practice, feedback and opportunity. The focus is on building useful skills and consistency rather than selling a lifestyle or shortcuts.
