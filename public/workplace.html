First 30 Days on the Job

Week 1: study the offer, customer profile, qualification rules, approved scripts, CRM and booking process. Observe experienced setters and practise safely.

Week 2: handle supervised conversations, update records, follow up responsibly and review quality with your manager.

Week 3: build consistency, handle objections, review reply and booking rates, and refine handoffs.

Week 4: run an organised daily workflow, review performance and agree development targets.

Practical standard operating procedures

Opening a DM conversation

Reference the prospect's context, ask one relevant question, listen to the answer and avoid a premature pitch. Follow employer outreach rules and platform policies.

Qualifying a prospect

Understand their goals, present situation, needs, timeline and relevant eligibility. Never invent answers or claim a lead is qualified without evidence.

Using a CRM

Open the correct contact, check prior messages, add factual notes, set an accurate pipeline stage and schedule the next permitted action.

Booking and handing off

Confirm interest, suitability, agreed time and timezone. Check calendar availability, record relevant context and hand over to the closer securely.

End-of-day reporting

Report activity and outcomes separately: conversations, replies, qualified leads, booked calls, known attendance and blockers. Never inflate metrics.

Cold calling

Prepare your approved opening, identify yourself, establish relevance, ask permission to continue where appropriate, qualify and record the outcome. Respect opt-outs and applicable privacy rules.

When results are poor

Review lead quality, offer fit, opening messages, reply rates, qualification quality, follow-up timing and client feedback before changing tactics.
