Three skills. One place to master them.

The simulator changes its behaviour based on what you say. Rush the conversation and trust drops. Ask the right questions and the prospect starts opening up.

01 / NETWORK

Build your network.

Talk with setters, closers, recruiters and operators. Learn to create relationships without immediately asking people for jobs or introductions.

02 / CLIENT ACQUISITION

Land clients.

Speak with business owners across different industries. Diagnose problems, create interest and earn the right to suggest a call.

03 / ON THE JOB

Handle real leads.

Practise inbound, warm and cold conversations. Handle uncertainty, price questions, objections and booking without sounding scripted.

NOT ANOTHER SCRIPT LIBRARY

The prospect reacts to you.

Every simulation has a hidden situation, motivation and objection. You aren't shown the answer. You have to uncover it through the conversation.
