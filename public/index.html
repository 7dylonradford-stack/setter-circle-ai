A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Good questions.
Clear answers.

All common questions ↗
Do I need sales experience?+

You can start with the foundations. The training builds from understanding appointment setting to practising conversations, handling objections and preparing for interviews.

What is the 40-Day Programme?+

A structured sequence of daily actions, including research, prospect lists, personalised outreach and conversation practice. It's a roadmap for consistent work, not a deadline for landing a job.

How does the practice work?+

Practise with fictional AI prospects, review feedback and repeat the skills you need to improve. Members also have a simulated workplace, interview preparation and mentor support.

Can I look around before joining?+

Yes. The Thinking of joining area includes a practice demo, the programme journey, founder introductions and student stories. Explore it here →
