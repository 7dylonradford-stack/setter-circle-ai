NOT ANOTHER SCRIPT LIBRARY

The prospect reacts to you.

Every simulation has a hidden situation, motivation and objection. You aren't shown the answer. You have to uncover it through the conversation.

✓
Hidden prospect stateTrust, interest and patience change throughout the conversation.
✓
Adaptive resistancePitch too early, ramble or ignore an objection and the prospect pushes back.
✓
Live coachingSee conversation signals while you train without revealing the prospect's hidden answer.
✓
Post-session debriefSee what was really happening and where your conversation could improve.
