Before joining · Your journey

What to expect from day one.

You're not simply buying access to videos. The Setter Circle combines structured learning, hands-on practice, consistent action and direct support to help you work towards your first appointment-setting role. Here's how the process fits together.

01
Start here · Skool Academy

Learn the foundations through the Skool course

Begin with the structured course on Skool. You'll learn what appointment setters actually do, how sales conversations work, how to find and approach suitable businesses, how to qualify prospects, handle objections and book appointments professionally. Work through the modules before expecting to perform confidently in live conversations.

02
Put theory into practice · Setter Hub

Train with the AI Roleplay Lab

Move into your member-only Setter Hub and practise conversations with simulated prospects. Work on opening messages, asking better questions, responding to objections and moving conversations forward. Use the AI feedback, scores and coaching tools to spot weaknesses, repeat scenarios and measure your progress. You can practise without putting a real client relationship at risk.

03
Build consistency · 40-Day Programme

Follow a structured 40-day action plan

Follow daily tasks that turn your learning into a routine. You'll research relevant businesses, build prospect lists, personalise outreach, practise conversations, review responses and improve your approach. The 40-day programme gives you structure and accountability; it is a training roadmap, not a promise that you will secure employment within 40 days.

04
Personal development · Mentor support

Work with Amar on your weaker areas

Alongside your independent practice, Amar helps you develop your skills through feedback, guided roleplays and support. You'll focus on the areas that matter most: communication, confidence, qualification, handling challenging replies and understanding what employers expect. Ask questions and use the community to keep improving rather than getting stuck.

05
Career preparation · Interview training

Get ready to apply and interview

Once you've developed the fundamentals, you'll prepare to present your skills to potential employers. Practise interview questions and realistic roleplay assessments with Amar, learn how to explain your experience honestly, and identify suitable appointment-setting, SDR or BDR opportunities. Your readiness depends on your progress, not just how many days have passed.

06
Your next step · Real opportunities

Apply, interview and work towards landing a role

Use your training, practice history and interview preparation to pursue suitable openings. The Setter Circle aims to support you with relevant opportunities and guidance as you apply, interview and improve from feedback. If you secure a role, the skills you've built can help you get started with greater confidence. Job offers, hiring decisions and earnings are determined by employers and your own performance.

How it all connects: Skool teaches the knowledge → the Setter Hub builds practical ability → the 40-Day Programme creates daily action → Amar provides mentoring and interview preparation → you pursue opportunities with stronger skills and support. These stages can overlap, and progress varies between students. No job or earnings are guaranteed.

Free beginner lesson

The first rule: understand before you pitch.

Here's one practical principle we teach beginners. Try it in the AI demo above.

Lesson 01 / Introduction

A message should start a conversation, not deliver a sales presentation.

A common beginner mistake is sending a long introduction, a list of services and a request to book a call — all before knowing whether the person needs help.

A better opening references something specific, asks one relevant question and gives the other person an easy way to reply.

Example: “Saw that your agency works with local businesses. Are most of your leads coming through referrals at the moment?”

You're trying to learn something useful, not win an argument.

Good questions matter

Know what you're joining.

We'd rather answer the difficult questions clearly than make promises we can't guarantee.

Do I need appointment-setting experience?

No. The programme is designed to introduce complete beginners to the fundamentals, although you'll need to practise consistently.

Does completing the course guarantee me a job?

No. There is no guaranteed role, income or specific placement date. Suitable opportunities depend on employer demand, your performance, availability and other factors.

How is the free AI demo different?

The public preview is a short four-turn scenario with basic illustrative feedback. Members have access to the broader roleplay and development platform, subject to their plan.

Can I ask questions without booking or buying?

Yes. Use the enquiry form or message our Instagram. A consultation is a chance to assess fit, not an obligation to enrol.

What careers can appointment-setting skills support?

Possible paths include DM-based appointment setting, lead generation, SDR and BDR roles. Requirements and compensation vary considerably between employers.
